# EIC paper-content-blind review commitment

Reviewer: internal AI conference-editor perspective; no manuscript content has been read for this commitment.

## Contract Paraphrase

Methodological sufficiency means transparent sampling, comparators, endpoints and uncertainty appropriate to the stated claim; it does not require every possible experiment.

Domain accuracy means established methods and cited studies are described correctly, with biomedical-imaging terminology and outcomes kept distinct.

Argumentative coherence means conclusions follow from the evidence actually presented, including unfavorable comparisons and pending experiments.

Cross-disciplinary relevance means adjacent biomedical engineers can identify the intended decision and understand the limits of translation.

Writing and structure mean a readable, self-contained short IEEE paper, legible tables/figures and compliance with the supplied venue template.

Venue contribution means a concrete original empirical or methodological insight of interest to EMBC; incrementality alone is not a reason for rejection.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
- dimension_id: D1
- what_to_look_for: design/denominators/comparators/uncertainty and reproducibility
- what_triggers_block: undisclosed confounding that invalidates central comparison
- what_triggers_warn: repairable missing design details or qualification
- what_triggers_fatal: fabricated methods or irrecoverable central design invalidity

### D2: domain_accuracy
- dimension_id: D2
- what_to_look_for: precise task, endpoint and citation descriptions
- what_triggers_block: material factual error changes the main conclusion
- what_triggers_warn: imprecise but repairable terminology/citation context
- what_triggers_fatal: invented central evidence or misrepresented core prior result

### D3: argumentative_coherence
- dimension_id: D3
- what_to_look_for: central question through results to conclusion
- what_triggers_block: main conclusion contradicts reported evidence
- what_triggers_warn: overbroad wording that can be narrowed without new data
- what_triggers_fatal: main argument cannot be made true from the presented evidence

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
- dimension_id: D4
- what_to_look_for: readership relevance, problem motivation and practical boundaries
- what_triggers_block: unsupported clinical efficacy presented as established
- what_triggers_warn: insufficient explanation for adjacent-field readers
- what_triggers_fatal: no fatal trigger solely for lack of clinical deployment

### D5: writing_and_structure
- dimension_id: D5
- what_to_look_for: abstract, organization, tables/figures, page/template fit
- what_triggers_block: unreadable or structurally incomplete manuscript
- what_triggers_warn: local clarity or format issues
- what_triggers_fatal: no fatal trigger solely for editable presentation

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
- dimension_id: D6
- what_to_look_for: one-sentence contribution and fair relation to prior work
- what_triggers_block: no identifiable contribution after reasonable reframing
- what_triggers_warn: contribution present but buried or overstated
- what_triggers_fatal: central contribution depends on fabricated novelty/evidence
