Key Policy Changes at a Glance
Paper Format and Length: A strict 4+1 page structure (up to 4 pages of technical content, plus a 5th page dedicated exclusively to references).
AI Use and Disclosure: Clear disclosure guidelines align with IEEE policy, strictly prohibiting unacknowledged generative AI use and prompt injection.
Reciprocal Review Requirement: Every submitted paper must designate a qualified author who agrees to serve as a reviewer for EMBC 2027 (unless a verified exemption applies).

Page Limits: Submissions are subject to a strict 5-page limit:
Pages 1–4: Technical text, figures, tables, and equations.
Page 5: Strictly reserved for acknowledgments and references only. No core technical content may appear on this page.
Reference Limit: References must not exceed 20 citations.
Originality: Submissions must describe original, unpublished work and cannot be under review elsewhere.
Author Cap: Each manuscript may list a maximum of 35 authors.
Language: All papers must be written in English.
Policy Adherence: All Papers submitted to EMBC must:
Be compliant with the IEEE Publications Service and Products Board Operations Manual and all policies therein
Be compliant with the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society Code of Ethics
Non-compliant submissions, including papers exceeding length constraints or altering template margins/fonts, will be desk-rejected without review.

At this world’s largest international biomedical engineering conference, a broad spectrum of tracks will cover cutting-edge research in biomedical devices, digital health, AI and data science, imaging, biosensors, robotics, and health informatics, alongside sessions dedicated to translation, regulation, and entrepreneurship.

Under this theme, the conference will bring together researchers, engineers, clinicians, policymakers, and industry partners to accelerate the journey from prototype to practice. We particularly welcome contributions that showcase successful academic–industry collaborations, translational pipelines, real-world deployments, and regulatory or reimbursement pathways for innovative technologies. In addition to distinguished keynotes, the program will feature workshops, mini-symposia, special sessions, oral and poster presentations, industry showcases, and events tailored for students, early-career investigators, and startups.
