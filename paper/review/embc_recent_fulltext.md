# Recent published EMBC comparators: full-text extraction

Date: 23 November 2026. Origin: deep-research/lit-review, delegated by academic-pipeline. This is a purposive narrative sample, not a proceedings census. Published-paper selection cannot estimate an acceptance probability or identify why reviewers accepted a submission. Full scientific author versions were read; version/access limits are explicit.

## A1. EffiSegNet — EMBC 2024

Vezakis, I. A., Georgas, K., Fotiadis, D., and Matsopoulos, G. K. *EffiSegNet: Gastrointestinal Polyp Segmentation through a Pre-Trained EfficientNet-based Network with a Simplified Decoder*. EMBC 2024, pp. 1–4. DOI [10.1109/EMBC53108.2024.10782015](https://doi.org/10.1109/EMBC53108.2024.10782015). [PubMed publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40039472/). [Full author PDF](https://www.dioptra-project.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Vezakis-et-al_EffiSegNet.pdf), four-page IEEE layout; [matching arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.16298).

**Extraction:** Kvasir-SEG: 1,000 endoscopic images, inherited 80/10/10 train/validation/test split. EfficientNet encoder, additive full-scale fusion and lightweight Ghost-module decoder; eight backbone variants plus a scratch-training ablation. Training: 300 epochs, nominal batch8, AdamW, Dice–cross-entropy. B4 F1=0.9552 versus DUCK-Net0.9502; mIoU0.9056 versus0.9051. Scratch B4 F1=0.9286. Reported comparisons mix their models with literature scores; no external test or seed/paired-CI analysis is presented. B7 overfits. **Locators:** §II/Fig.1, p.2; §III, TablesI–III, p.3; §VI–VII, p.4. Clinical motivation is polyp delineation, without a prospective clinical intervention.

**Interpretation for Exchange-ML:** This publication is evidence that a focused engineering change, one established dataset, a modest strongest-baseline point gain and an informative ablation can constitute an EMBC paper. It does not establish that those properties are sufficient for acceptance. Exchange-ML should retain strong component controls and show a sharply defined contribution; it need not invent a larger network to appear substantive. The paper's unreported paired uncertainty is not permission for us to remove our confidence intervals. Its pretrained-versus-scratch comparison also warns that apparent architectural benefit can reflect another changed factor.

## A2. MI-CES — EMBC 2025

Thomas, A., Cho, Y., Zhao, H., and Carlson, T. *MI-CES: An explainable weak labelling approach to example selection for Motor Imagery BCI classification*. EMBC 2025, pp.1–7. DOI [10.1109/EMBC58623.2025.11253265](https://doi.org/10.1109/EMBC58623.2025.11253265). [PubMed publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41335965/). [Full accepted author manuscript](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10208047/1/EMBC_Matched_filtering_ACCEPTED.pdf), seven pages with explicit acceptance header.

**Extraction:** Three datasets: BCI CompetitionIV2a (nine participants), PhysioNet BCI2000 (described as over100 participants), BCI-VR (session-wise evaluation); exact analyzed totals for the latter two are not clearly enumerated in the inspected text. Left/right imagery uses15 common channels. Neurophysiological synthetic-template covariance similarity selects epochs at normalized score>0.5. Compare all-versus-selected examples using CSP/linear-SVM and FFT/RBF-SVM, five-fold CV and Wilcoxon signed-rank tests. Results are mixed: FFT significant on IV2a, CSP significant on PhysioNet, both on BCI-VR; some participants worsen. Numerical mean accuracy differences are not tabulated, so none are inferred from boxplots. Online feedback benefit remains untested. **Locators:** §III-A/B, p.3; §IV, p.4; §V–VI/Figs.7–13/TableI, pp.5–6; future-work discussion/conclusion, p.7.

**Interpretation for Exchange-ML:** Simple, domain-motivated selection can be a recognizable contribution without deep architecture novelty. Importantly, acceptance/publication coexists with mixed subgroup/model findings and a clinical-use hypothesis reserved for future work. This supports an honest analysis paper or targeted QC policy with explicit limits. Classification on selected examples changes the analyzed population, so it is not a template for claiming equal-budget deployment benefit; Exchange-ML must keep all-case denominators and fixed CT review cost.

## A3. Few-shot eczema — EMBC 2024, author-preprint version caveat

Kumar, N., Aran, O., and Vasudevan, V. *Visual In-Context Learning for Few-Shot Eczema Segmentation*. Published EMBC2024, pp.1–5, DOI [10.1109/EMBC53108.2024.10781985](https://doi.org/10.1109/EMBC53108.2024.10781985). [PubMed publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40039380/). Full scientific text read: [arXiv2309.16656v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.16656), ten-page2023 author preprint. **This is not a retrieved final five-page proceedings PDF.** Same authors/title and headline scores are independently confirmed in the EMBC publication abstract; detailed extraction below belongs to the author preprint, not a verified unchanged final version. Do not infer accepted-paper page limits from its length.

**Extraction:** 528 eczema images from Dermnet and an in-house consumer study; 428 training/100 evaluation images. Patient-level independence is not specified. Pretrained SegGPT retrieves nearest training examples using SSIM/Frobenius distance, varying k1–15. Its best reported k2/SSIM mIoU36.69 versus U-Net32.60; U-Net trains50 epochs. All428 images form the retrieval bank despite two prompts per query. More prompts can hurt. No repeated-seed/paired CI, independent external cohort or demonstrated demographic-fairness gain is reported. **Locators in preprint:** §2/Fig.1, pp.3–4; §3.1–3.3, pp.4–5; §3.4/Table1/Figs.2–4, pp.5–7; §4, p.8.

**Interpretation for Exchange-ML:** A clear small-data question and an accessible baseline comparison are recognizable EMBC themes. The relevant design lesson is to test deployment-relevant selection, not to assume more samples/models/features must help. This author-version comparison does not establish current segmentation-SOTA requirements or validate a claim that only two annotated images are needed overall. It also does not establish that our weak fusion gain is enough: our strongest story must come from a distinct mechanism or evaluation finding.

## Cross-paper implications and limits

These three published identities span segmentation architecture, deployment-oriented few-shot selection and explainable BCI sample selection. They support a **practical target**, not an inferred acceptance threshold: one bounded question, understandable biomedical relevance, concrete baseline comparisons, a mechanism/ablation and claims proportionate to the measured evaluation. Large multicenter clinical studies and victory on every subgroup are not universal properties of this small sample. Conversely, lack of those features in accepted papers is not evidence that rigor is dispensable.

For Exchange-ML, the strongest near-term improvement is to make the existing contribution explicit: characterize presence/partition behavior and quantify endpoint-dependent ranking. Negative fusion results can be a disciplined ablation. An operational omission policy needs independent or explicitly exploratory assessment; substantial clinical-review savings cannot be inferred from point AURC improvements. Do not lower P1, omit failures, recast viewed cases as untouched confirmation, or import pending E6 effects into the manuscript.
